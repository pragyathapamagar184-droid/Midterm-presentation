About Magar Community

The Magar people are one of the largest indigenous ethnic groups of Nepal, with a rich history rooted in the western hills. Known for their bravery, rich folklore, traditional music, and vibrant agricultural lifestyle, Magars have contributed significantly to Nepal's diverse identity.

Culture & Traditions

Maghe Sankranti

The biggest festival of the Magar community, celebrated with traditional foods like Sel Roti, Tarul (yam), and cultural gatherings.

Kauda & Salaijo

Kauda and Salaijo play an essential role in preserving the oral history, language, and social values of the Magar and Gurung communities.

Traditional Dress

Men wear Bhangra and Kachhad, while women dress in colorful Gunyou Cholo, Mujetro, and traditional gold jewelry.
